How each report is placed

  1. One report per id. The export repeats some rows, so the table counts each id once.
  2. The cause comes from the tenant, not from the intake tag or the subject line (causes.ts): the locale the tenant writes dates in, the booking form they run, and how the report came in.
  3. A report the desk read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) is its own problem. Swapping day and month cannot move a date by one day.

parser: the shared parser reads day-first dates month first

Every tenant in #4471 writes the day before the month. The shared parser reads every date month first, so a booking for 7 April goes out for 4 July. It comes from every tenant, by app, by email and through booking-v2’s form. This is the fix that is queued.

hint: booking-v3 tells day-first tenants to type month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at a day-first tenant type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The date comes out flipped, as with the parser, but only through booking-v3’s form. The parser fix does not touch it. Before this page it had no ticket of its own.

one-day: dates move by one day

These reports do not swap day and month. The date moves by one day:

  • “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.”
  • “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.”

The day depends on where the reader is: a time zone bug, not a date format bug. The kb counted these under parser by tenant and left them in a footnote. The parser fix does not touch them either.

For the desk

  • Place a report by its cause, not by its subject line or its intake tag. Two matching subjects do not make one cause (decision 0001).
  • When a report does not show a swapped day and month, read it before you merge it.
