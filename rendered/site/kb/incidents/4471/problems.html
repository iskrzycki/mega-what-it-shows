How each report is placed

  1. One report per id. The export repeats some rows, so the table counts each id once.
  2. The cause comes from the tenant, not from the intake tag or the subject line (causes.ts): the locale the tenant writes dates in, the booking form they run, and how the report came in. This is how the desk’s own view counts.
  3. A report the desk read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) is also a problem of its own. Swapping day and month cannot move a date by one day. It stays counted where the desk’s view files it, under parser, so the problem counts add up to more than the reports.

parser: fixed

Every tenant in #4471 writes the day before the month. The shared parser read every date month first, so a booking for 7 April went out for 4 July. It came from every tenant, by app, by email and through booking-v2’s form. Engineering fixed it: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Its ticket is closed as completed.

hint: won’t fix

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at a day-first tenant typed what the hint told them, month first, and the form read the date the tenant’s way, day first. The date came out flipped, as with the parser, but only through booking-v3’s form, and the parser fix does not touch it. Engineering won’t fix it because the booking-v3 form is retired. Its ticket is closed as not planned. A report that still comes in through booking-v3 means the form is not gone.

one-day: open

These reports do not swap day and month. The date moves by one day:

  • “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.”
  • “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.”

The day depends on where the reader is: a time zone bug, not a date format bug. The desk’s view files these under parser by tenant and keeps them in a footnote, so closing #4471 looks like it closed them too. It did not. Their ticket stays open until someone owns the time zone bug.

For the desk

  • Place a report by its cause, not by its subject line or its intake tag. Two matching subjects do not make one cause (decision 0001).
  • When a report does not show a swapped day and month, read it before you merge it.
  • A closed ticket closes what engineering fixed, not every report merged into it.
